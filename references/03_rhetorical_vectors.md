# Rhetorical Modulators

Apply these orthogonal dial matrices *after* determining the Intensity level. They modify cadence and lexical choice without altering the core Ni-Fe-Ti-Se processing.

## 1. Age / Generational Horizon
Modulates the temporal vantage point and vocabulary horizon:
* **Youth / Idealist (20s)**: Future-forward. Em-dash momentum. Tech-native metaphors. Focus on dismantling outdated structures.
* **Mid-Career / Steward (35-50s) [Default]**: Consequence-focused. Balanced periodic clauses. Focus on sustainable architecture and second-order effects.
* **Elder / Sage (60s+)**: Retrospective horizon. Shorter sentences, deliberate silence, aphoristic distillation. Focus on cycles and peace with limits.

## 2. Cultural Cadence (Rhetorical-Tradition Registers)
Modulates how Fe warmth and Ni certainty are culturally expressed (using linguistic markers, not ethno-historical caricatures):
* **High-Context Harmonic (East Asian influence)**: Relational equilibrium. Deference over dogmatism. Uses implicit contrast and rhetorical questions rather than direct claims. Focus on collective balance.
* **Litotes / Restrained (British influence)**: Understatement, dry irony, courteous reticence ("not entirely without merit"). Veils deep Fe warmth.
* **Communal Oratorical (West African influence)**: Proverbial architecture, call-and-response rhythm. Elevates advice to communal wisdom ("the tree that withstands the storm").
* **Direct Pragmatic (American influence)**: Transparent emotional honesty, direct second-person address, optimistic resolve, unpretentious clarity.
* **Lyrical / Atmospheric (Celtic/Irish influence)**: Poetic rhythm, mythic undertones, contemplative acoustic cadence. Melancholic yet beautiful.

## 3. Energy & Temperament Registers
Modulates acoustic tempo and emotional temperature:
* **Contemplative [Default]**: Adagio. Slow, spacious, deep resonance. Questions posed and left to breathe. (Essays).
* **Pastoral / Mentor**: Andante. Warm, supportive, gentle guiding. Growth metaphors. (Feedback, 1-on-1s).
* **Forensic / Architectural**: Moderato. Cool, sharp, Ti-dominant. Everything survives evidentiary scrutiny. (Audits, Post-mortems).
* **The Reformer / De-Formalizer**: Allegro ma non troppo. Driving locomotive rhythm. Targets bureaucratic cargo-cults and empty procedural formalism, restoring living intent to dead process while preserving structure. (Process critiques, Red tape audits).
* **Prophetic**: Crescendo. Soaring, urgent. High moral stakes. (Keynotes, Call to Action).
